# Spotify data donation — consent (v1.0)

By submitting your Spotify data export, you agree that the University of Melbourne
research team may store and analyse the contents of those files for the research
purposes described in the donor information sheet you received with your
participant code.

You may withdraw your data at any time before publication by contacting the
research team and quoting your participant code.

Your participant code is the only identifier stored with your data. The research
team does not receive your name, email, or any other personal information through
this donation flow.
